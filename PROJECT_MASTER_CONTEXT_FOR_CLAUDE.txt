1. EXECUTIVE RESEARCH OVERVIEW & CORE HYPOTHESIS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Content-Based Image Retrieval (CBIR) on edge-computing devices and IoT micro-servers is constrained
by severe memory, latency, and power budgets. Heavy deep neural networks (e.g., ResNet-50,
EfficientNet) and Vision-Language Models (e.g., CLIP) achieve high retrieval accuracy but incur
prohibitive storage overhead (2,048 to 8,192 bytes per image descriptor), high extraction latencies
(10 to 25 ms per image), and compute-intensive matrix multiplications.

The Core Proposed System (M5) introduces an ultra-compact, query-adaptive hybrid CBIR architecture:
- 13-Dimensional Descriptor: 9 statistical colour moments across the HSV colour space combined with
  4 Grey-Level Co-occurrence Matrix (GLCM) texture moments.
- Adaptive Metric-Learning MLP: A 2-layer feed-forward network (13 -> 16 -> 8 -> 2) with LeakyReLU
  activations and a final Softmax constraint, producing query-specific weights [wC(q), wT(q)].
- Total Trainable Parameters: 378 weights/biases (450 total tensor elements, 1.76 KB file size).
- Objective Function: Batch-hard triplet margin loss (margin m = 0.15) trained with Adam (lr = 1e-3).
- Strict Unit Simplex Constraint: wC(q) + wT(q) = 1.0, with wC(q), wT(q) > 0.
- Retrieval Metric: Weighted Euclidean distance:
    D_M5(q, g) = sqrt( wC(q) * sum((f_q,i^C - f_g,i^C)^2) + wT(q) * sum((f_q,j^T - f_g,j^T)^2) )
- Edge Performance Niche:
    * Descriptor Storage: 52 bytes/image (157.5x smaller than ResNet-50, 39.4x smaller than CLIP).
    * Extraction Latency: 0.45 ms/image on CPU (53.4x faster than ResNet-50, 36.9x faster than CLIP).
    * Query Inference: 0.0827 ms per query forward pass on CPU.
    * Database Ranking: Top-10 ranking over 899 images in 0.0087 ms; 7,000 images in 0.477 ms (linear O(N)).

2. EVALUATED MODEL TAXONOMY (M1 TO M6, D1 TO D5)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Handcrafted Models Evaluated on Corel-1K:
- M1 (Colour Only): 9-D HSV statistical moments, Euclidean distance.
- M2 (Texture Only): 4-D GLCM texture moments, Euclidean distance.
- M3 (Equal Feature Fusion): 13-D hybrid vector with fixed 0.5 colour / 0.5 texture weighting.
- M4 (Heuristic Fixed Fusion): 13-D hybrid vector with fixed 0.7 colour / 0.3 texture weighting.
- M5 (Proposed Adaptive MLP): 13-D hybrid vector with dynamic query-dependent weights [wC(q), wT(q)].
- M6 (Mean Weight Control): 13-D hybrid vector with fixed weights set to the dataset-wide mean of M5
  predicted weights (wC = 0.578, wT = 0.422). Serves as a vital negative control to prove whether
  dynamic query-level adaptation yields benefits beyond static distance re-scaling.

Baselines Evaluated (Phase 8):
- D1: Local Binary Patterns (LBP, 10-D uniform histogram, Chi-Square distance).
- D2: Pretrained ResNet-50 (2048-D penultimate avgpool layer, ImageNet-1K, Cosine distance).
- D3: Pretrained EfficientNet-B0 (1280-D features, ImageNet-1K, Cosine distance).
- D4: Fixed Handcrafted Fusions (M3, M4, M6).
- D5: Pretrained CLIP ViT-B/32 (512-D visual embeddings, LAION-2B, Cosine distance).

3. DATASET PROTOCOL & ZERO-LEAKAGE PARTITIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary Benchmark: Wang Corel-1K (1,000 images across 10 semantic classes: Africa, Beaches,
Buildings, Buses, Dinosaurs, Elephants, Flowers, Horses, Mountains, Food).
- Seed: 42 (strictly fixed across all splits, mini-batch shuffling, and evaluation).
- Held-out Query Partition: 100 images (exactly 10 per category).
- Internal Training Partition: 700 images (70 per category).
- Internal Validation Partition: 199 images (20 per category, except Horses with 19).
- Purged Duplicate: 1 image (horses/761.jpg excluded due to identical perceptual hash to query horses/760.jpg).
- Benchmark Gallery Size: 899 images (700 train + 199 val).
- Strict Zero-Leakage Protocol: Feature normalization (Min-Max) was computed strictly on the 899 gallery
  images (train_min, train_max). Held-out query features were scaled strictly using these gallery bounds.
  Zero query statistics were ever exposed during normalization or training.

External Transfer Benchmark (Phase 9): Oxford-IIIT Pet Dataset (7,390 images across 37 breeds of cats
and dogs: 12 cat breeds, 25 dog breeds; 370 held-out queries, 6,979 gallery images).
- Protocol: Strictly zero-shot cross-dataset transfer using the frozen Corel-trained MLP model. No fine-tuning.

PHASE 5: CORRECTED COREL-1K BENCHMARK EVALUATION
- Full evaluation of M1 to M6 on 899 gallery images and 100 held-out queries.
- Exact metric formulation:
    * Precision@10: Fraction of top-10 retrieved images belonging to query category.
    * Recall@10: Fraction of all relevant gallery images retrieved in top-10.
    * F1@10: Calculated at the per-query level using unrounded precision and recall, then averaged:
        F1_query = 2 * (P * R) / (P + R + eps), Mean_F1 = mean(F1_query).
        (Avoids the harmonic mean aggregation fallacy of computing harmonic mean of aggregate P and R).
    * Mean Average Precision (mAP): Computed over full 899-image gallery ranking.

6. CRITICAL TECHNICAL PITFALLS ENCOUNTERED & EXACT SOLUTIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Windows cp1252 Terminal Crash:
   - Issue: Unicode characters (arrows, deltas, subset symbols) crashed scripts on Windows PowerShell.
   - Solution: Enforced pure ASCII console logging and UTF-8 file encoding throughout all pipelines.

2. NumPy 2.x `allow_pickle=False` Error:
   - Issue: Loading pre-computed cache dictionaries with `np.load` crashed on NumPy 2.4+.
   - Solution: Explicitly passed `allow_pickle=True` in all `np.load()` calls.

3. Official Oxford University VGG Server Timeout:
   - Issue: The official Oxford VGG server timed out during dataset download.
   - Solution: Built a custom multi-threaded chunked downloader (`download_oxford_pet.py`) connecting to
     the fastai Amazon S3 mirror, downloading 16 concurrent byte ranges in 350 seconds.

4. Windows Paging File / MemoryError on `ProcessPoolExecutor`:
   - Issue: Spawning 24 Python worker processes simultaneously importing PyTorch DLLs exhausted the
     Windows paging file.
   - Solution: Replaced `ProcessPoolExecutor` with `ThreadPoolExecutor(max_workers=8)`, which releases the
     GIL during C-level PIL and skimage image processing without multi-process DLL overhead.

5. PyTorch Checkpoint Loading KeyError:
   - Issue: Checkpoint contained a dictionary with `"model_state_dict"`, causing direct weight load failure.
   - Solution: Unpacked `ckpt["model_state_dict"]` before calling `model.load_state_dict()`.

6. F-String Curly Brace Interpolation Error:
   - Issue: LaTeX expressions like `\mathcal{O}(N)` and `{csv,tex}` inside Python f-strings caused `NameError: name 'O' is not defined`.
   - Solution: Converted report templates to standard triple-quoted strings with `.replace()` placeholders.

7. F1 Harmonic Mean Fallacy:
   - Issue: Calculating aggregate F1 as harmonic mean of aggregate P and aggregate R biases the metric.
   - Solution: Calculated F1 strictly at the per-query level using unrounded P and R before averaging across queries.

7. MANDATORY RESEARCH INTEGRITY DISCLOSURES (FOR MANUSCRIPT REVISION)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When writing or revising the manuscript for ICAIN 2026, adhere strictly to these empirical facts:
1. Statistically Significant Results:
   - Proposed M5 is statistically significantly superior to classical LBP texture (D1, p = 6.61e-9)
     and heuristic fixed fusion (M4, p = 5.35e-8).
2. Descriptive-Only Results:
   - The numerical improvements of M5 over equal fusion M3 (+0.0148 mAP) and mean control M6 (+0.0193 mAP)
     are NOT statistically significant after Holm-Bonferroni correction (p = 0.6519).
3. Category Limitation (Flowers Driver):
   - M5's advantage over fixed controls is heavily concentrated in the Flowers category (+0.2103 mAP gain).
     Excluding Flowers, the mean paired difference across the remaining 9 categories is -0.00197.
4. Gallery Sensitivity Invariant:
   - Smaller galleries mechanically inflate recall and F1 due to a smaller denominator (|Rel| shrinks).
     This must be explained as a mathematical consequence of gallery size, not superior retrieval capability.
5. Deep-Learning Baseline Reality:
   - Deep neural networks (ResNet-50: 0.9103, EfficientNet-B0: 0.8879, CLIP: 0.9485) dominate semantic accuracy.
   - The paper's scientific merit lies strictly in its ultra-low resource niche: 52 bytes/image, 0.45 ms CPU latency,
     and 1.76 KB model footprint for edge devices.
6. Zero-Shot Transfer Boundary:
   - Transfer to fine-grained Oxford-IIIT Pet yields ~3.33% mAP (near random chance ~2.7%), establishing that
     global moments cannot distinguish fine-grained biological breeds without deep local spatial geometry.
